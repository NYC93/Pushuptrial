    How to set up
    1. Stick bright tape (e.g. yellow) on the side of the chest facing the camera.
    2. Phone in landscape, 2–3 m away, side-on, at floor height. Whole body (ear to ankle) in frame.
    3. Tap Pick colour, then tap the tape in the video.
    4. Set the target line (drag, or bottom position + Line @ marker).
    5. A rep counts when the tape touches the line while all enabled joints are in range, then the student pushes back up.
    Knee/hip/neck angles are measured on the floor side: above 180° = sagging, below 180° = piking (neck: below 180° = head dropping).
